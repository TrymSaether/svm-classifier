# General comments
Your report so far looks very nice, with a very clean looking template! There is a lot of good here! I have some notes on what should be improved. Also, you need to add some numerical results to the final report.

Comments on presentation: Your presentation is really nice so far. I really like the boxes used for presenting the theorems and proofs. But you should probably fix the problem where you get almost a whole empty page after proposition 1(I assume this is related to the proof needing to be in one chunk?). 

Comments on theory: Your proof of existence for the primal problem is not quite correct, and also a bit too short. Using coercivity to argue for existence is mainly applicable for unrestricted optimisation problems, which this is not. 
Your claims relating to uniqueness for the primal problem are not quite correct. Strict convexity in one variable does not necessarily imply that there is a unique optimiser(for restricted problems). You need to also discuss the feasible set. Look for instance at minimizing x2
for x∈(∞,−1]∪[1,∞).  Here, the solution is not unique, even though the objective function is strictly convex. You should also make the note that any local optimiser is also a global(though not necessarily unique). 

Theorem 2 is good as presented, with a good proof. However, it would make more sense to move this to after the derivation of the dual problem. Meaning, you first derive the dual problem from the primal problem. Then you discuss existence and uniqueness. The footnotes on page 4 are a little bit unnecessary. These definitions are from the curriculum, and it is fair to assume the reader knows these definitions in this case. While your proposition 1 is on the whole correct with a good proof, the statement "The dual problem is equivalent to the primal problem" seems incorrect to me, or you should at least clarify what is meant by equivalent here.
